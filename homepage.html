Frequently Asked Questions

What courses do you offer?

We offer nationally recognised vocational courses, including:

  • CHC30125 Certificate III in Early Childhood Education and Care
  • CPC50220 Diploma of Building and Construction (Building)
  • CPC40120 Certificate IV in Building and Construction
  • SHB30516 Certificate III in Barbering (Apprenticeship)
  • SHB30516 Certificate III in Barbering (Non-Apprenticeship)
  • SHB30416 Certificate III in Hairdressing (Apprenticeship)
  • SHB30416 Certificate III in Hairdressing (Non-Apprenticeship)
Who can apply for your courses?

Our courses are open to school leavers, job seekers, career changers, apprentices, and anyone looking to gain practical skills and nationally recognised qualifications.

Do I need previous experience before enrolling?

No. Many of our programs are designed for beginners. Some courses may have specific entry requirements, and our admissions team can help you determine the best course for your background and career goals.

What is the difference between apprenticeship and non-apprenticeship courses?

Apprenticeship courses combine workplace employment with formal training, allowing students to earn while they learn. Non-apprenticeship courses are ideal for individuals who want to study full-time or part-time without being employed as an apprentice.

Will I receive a nationally recognised qualification?

Yes. Students who successfully complete their course will receive the relevant nationally recognised qualification, subject to meeting all course requirements.

Are your courses practical?

Yes. Our training combines classroom learning with hands-on practical experience, helping students develop the skills and confidence required in real workplaces.

What career opportunities are available after completing these courses?

Graduates may pursue careers such as:

  • Site Supervisor
  • Construction Manager
  • Building Estimator
  • Barber
  • Hairdresser
  • Salon Professional
  • Small Business Owner

Career opportunities depend on the qualification completed and relevant industry requirements.

What is Nationally Recognised Training?

Nationally Recognised Training is vocational education and training delivered by a Registered Training Organisation (RTO) and recognised across Australia. Successfully completing a nationally recognised course means you receive a qualification or Statement of Attainment that is accepted by employers and other education providers nationwide.

Fran Capelli Academy delivers industry-focused, nationally recognised training to help students build real skills and successful careers.
